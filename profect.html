Facility Requirements

Power:
125/208V, 3-phase with Neutral & Ground, 12.5 KW maximum load
Cord:
10-5 SO cord, 10 ft. length
Receptacle:
Hubbell 8461 plug, 60A, 3-phase, 250V, 3P-5W
Water:
1/4″ flexible hose with shut-off valve; replaceable-cartridge filter; no backflow preventor or drain required
Ventilation:
12″ diameter hood on 8″ galvanized vertical vent pipe; 350–400 CFM updraft blower recommended (Dayton – Grainger PN 7DF67); install hood 3–5″ above roaster cyclone exhaust

Current Sensor Control required to power exhaust blower externally (recommended: SSAC-ECS41BC – Grainger PN 6C055)
Certifications:
ETL Listed: UL-197, CSA C22.2 STD 109
