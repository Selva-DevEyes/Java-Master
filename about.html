Engineering, Craft and a Better Cup

Java Master brings an engineering mindset to air roasting—and a partnership mindset to the businesses that use it.

The beginning

A Smoother Cup Inspired a New Kind of Roaster

Java Master’s published story begins in 1987, when thermodynamic engineers looking for smooth coffee built a fluid-bed air roaster. The approach uses controlled hot air to move and roast the beans while separating chaff.

That engineering foundation developed into commercial equipment designed around repeatability, visible roasting and a cleaner-tasting result.

Why air

Balanced, Not Burnt

Java Master positions air roasting as an alternative to direct-fire drum roasting, using controlled convection and a climate-managed internal environment.

01

Uniform Movement

Air keeps beans moving through the chamber as heat is applied.

02

Chaff Removal

The process separates bitter outer chaff during the roast.

03

Controlled Variables

Insulation and measured conditions support more repeatable outcomes.

04

Electric Heat

Java Master roasters use electricity rather than a gas flame to heat the beans.

05

Theater of the Roast

The glass chamber turns movement, color and aroma into part of the experience.
